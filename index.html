Current Work

I build CHNGMKR, a peer-reviewed map of changemakers: people and projects whose work has measurably changed a system, nominated by their peers. Curators, recognized figures from very different fields and regions, nominate the projects and the people they know or admire. The map covers the transitions that condition all the others: energy and climate, living systems and land, food, health, education and knowledge, democratic institutions, work and the economy, culture, and more.

The map is free and shows that these people exist, concretely, in a place, in a city. On top of it, CHNGMKR organizes introductions so they can meet and act together. Organizations pay for introductions; half of each fee goes to the changemaker and the curator.

The corpus holds 1,000 documented profiles of people and projects; 250 are published after curator review. Fifteen curators are active, among them Rob Hopkins, founder of the Transition Towns movement; Søren Hermansen, pioneer of the Samsø energy transition; Katy Foraz, head of research at CERN’s LHC; Daria Robinson; Olivier Roellinger and Pierre Rosanvallon. Two contracts are signed: a curated-encounters agreement with Voyageurs du Monde, and a territorial deployment of the map for Bpifrance and Région Guadeloupe under France 2030, live at guadeloupe.chngmkr.com.

Previously

  • in:Expeditions (2017–). Founded in 2017, run with Coralie Custos Quatreville. Learning expeditions centred on impact for executive committees, future board members and family businesses, low-carbon by design and mostly in Europe, guided by changemakers.
  • Tomorrow! (2017–2020). Co-founder. Extension of Europe Tomorrow to the world: a program to send 30 young explorers across five continents to identify and document social innovations, who then guided executives and public decision-makers on inspiration and exploration journeys. Launched Brasil Tomorrow and Caribbean Tomorrow (Guadeloupe).
  • Europe Tomorrow (2014–2017). Co-founder, with Boris Marcel and Malo Richard, with the support of Michel Barnier, then European Commissioner, and in partnership with the European Commission. One year on the roads of Europe to map social innovation: 2,500 initiatives identified in 20 countries, 300 published in 12 categories, a study on the economic impact of social and sustainable innovation in the EU, and a case for a European Social and Sustainable Innovation Act. Bridged field initiatives with L’Oréal, Hitachi, McCain and the European Commission through knowledge expeditions and inspirational getaways.
  • Carrotmob France (2010–2014). Brought the Carrotmob movement to France at 18, with the agreement of its founder Brent Schulkin: the first community of responsible consumers in France, rewarding shops for their sustainable practices through organized “buycotts”. First operation in Rochefort-sur-Mer gathered more than 250 consumers in one café, whose owner committed the proceeds to energy savings. By 20, a national network: some forty French cities were organizing carrotmobs. Then founded a communication agency in Rennes built on the same idea.

Background

Grew up in Paimpol, Brittany; then Rennes. Lived in New York. Now based in Paris. Explorer-entrepreneur: I travel to meet the people reinventing the world at every level, and I have spent fifteen years turning those encounters into maps, expeditions and, now, a platform.
